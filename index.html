About

Every frame
is a feeling.

I'm Saurav Yadav — a cinematographer and video editor obsessed with the poetry of light, motion, and narrative. I believe the camera isn't just a tool; it's a language. My work spans short films, brand stories, and documentary work, always with a singular intent: to make the viewer feel something real.

With a meticulous eye for color grading and a deep understanding of visual rhythm, I craft cinematic experiences that linger long after the screen goes dark.
